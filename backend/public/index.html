Our Mission

CIEPD's Conflict Watch Center uses community intelligence and advanced conflict indicators to identify, track, and monitor conflict hotspots in the Niger Delta.

We are prevention-focused, targeting early warning rather than crisis response. Our approach analyzes situations, events, and actors with capacity to incite violence.
