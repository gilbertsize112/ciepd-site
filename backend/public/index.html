Our Mission & Vision

The CIEPD Conflict Watch Center leverages community-based intelligence and comprehensive conflict indicators to identify, track, monitor, and report on potential conflict hotspots in the Niger Delta region.

Our approach is preventive-focused, targeting early warning mechanisms rather than crisis mitigation. We analyze situations, events, and actors—individual, group, and institutional—with the capacity to incite violence.
