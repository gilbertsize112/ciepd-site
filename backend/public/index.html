Our Mission

Prevention-First Conflict Intelligence

CIEPD's Conflict Watch Center leverages community intelligence and advanced conflict indicators to identify, track, and monitor hotspots across the Niger Delta — before violence erupts.

We are prevention-focused, targeting early warning rather than crisis response. Our methodology analyzes situations, events, and actors with the capacity to incite or escalate violence — providing actionable intelligence to communities, government, and civil society.

Our Process

How the Watch Center Works

A three-step intelligence pipeline from field report to community protection.

01
Collect Intelligence
Community members, field reporters, and partner networks submit incident reports via web, mobile, or WhatsApp. All submissions are encrypted and securely stored.
02
Analyze & Verify
Our analysts and AI engine cross-reference incoming reports, identify patterns, verify claims, and assign severity ratings to each incident.
03
Alert & Respond
Verified intelligence is published and pushed to subscribers, government agencies, and peace-building partners to enable rapid, targeted intervention.

Our Journey

Building Peace Since 2017

2017
CIEPD founded — launched conflict monitoring with community partners across the Niger Delta, establishing the foundation for a community-driven intelligence network.
2020
Digital platform launched — enabling online report submissions and expanding the reporter network to over 200 active community contributors across 6 states.
2022
Scaled to 50+ communities with real-time alert systems and SMS notifications, significantly reducing average response time to emerging conflicts.
2024
Deployed advanced AI analytics engine, interactive live map, and admin intelligence dashboard — transforming CIEPD into a full-spectrum conflict monitoring platform.
