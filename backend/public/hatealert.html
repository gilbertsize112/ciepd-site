DUAL-MATCH FILTER RULE (ACTIVE)
An article is flagged ONLY when it satisfies ALL THREE of the following conditions:
  ① Nigeria Location: Article headline must contain a Nigerian location term (e.g. Nigeria, Lagos, Abuja…)
  ② Niger Delta Region: Article headline OR body must mention a Niger Delta / South-South term
  ③ Threat Content: Article must also contain at least one conflict keyword OR one hate speech keyword
Sports, entertainment, business and general news from Nigeria with no violence/hate content will be silently ignored.
